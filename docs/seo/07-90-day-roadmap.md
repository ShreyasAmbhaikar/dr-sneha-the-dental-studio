## Decision rules

- **Day 30, no movement on any keyword:** re-check NAP on the site, GBP and the first 15 citations; confirm the categories saved (Google sometimes rejects edits silently); check for a suspension or "edits pending" notice.
- **Rank drops >2 places after a category batch:** remove the last category added; re-scan in 7 days.
- **Reviews <10 in a month:** move the WhatsApp request to the same hour as treatment completion; add a reception script.
- **Day 90 in top 3 for the scan keyword but not for "dentist near me":** shift effort to links and neighbourhood pages (prominence), and to reviews mentioning Kharadi and Viman Nagar.
