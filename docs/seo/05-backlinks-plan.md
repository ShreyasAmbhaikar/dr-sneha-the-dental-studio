# 05 · Backlink plan

Target: **4–6 genuinely local links a month** — 3 in Dec 2026, 5 in Jan 2027, 6/month from Feb. Local relevance beats volume: one link from a Wadgaon Sheri society or a Pune news site outweighs dozens of generic SEO links.

## Press date hooks (next 6 months)

- **17 Dec 2026** — clinic's 8th anniversary (opened 17 Dec 2018): free check-up week for residents.
- **Jan 2027** — New Year oral-health habits; corporate camp in Kharadi.
- **20 Mar 2027** — World Oral Health Day: free camp in Wadgaon Sheri.
- **May–Jun 2027** — before schools reopen: kids' dental check-up drive.

## Outreach templates

**Society / school camp**
> Subject: Free dental check-up camp for {Society/School} residents
> Namaste {Name}, I'm Dr. Sneha Sardar from Dr. Sneha-The Dental Studio, opposite Datta Mandir in Wadgaon Sheri. We'd like to hold a free 2-hour dental check-up camp for {residents/students} on a date that suits you. There's no cost or obligation. If it's useful, we'd be grateful for a short mention on your website or notice board afterwards. Could we find a date in {month}?

**Health partner**
> Hello Dr. {Name}, we're a family dental clinic in Wadgaon Sheri and often see patients who'd benefit from {paediatric/gynae/skin} care. Would you be open to a mutual referral arrangement and listing each other on our websites' "trusted local partners" pages?

**Press**
> Subject: Wadgaon Sheri dental clinic marks 8 years with free check-up week
> {2-paragraph story, one quote from Dr. Sneha Sardar, photo, contact.}

## Where links should point

- Brand / press / anniversary → home page.
- Camps for children → `/treatments/kids-dentistry`; corporate camps → `/dentist-in-kharadi`.
- Partner pages → the most relevant treatment page.
- Videos → the treatment they explain.

## Avoid

Paid link packages, PBNs, mass directory blasts beyond the citation plan, foreign guest-post farms, link exchanges with unrelated sites, exact-match anchor text on every link (use the clinic name or natural phrases).
