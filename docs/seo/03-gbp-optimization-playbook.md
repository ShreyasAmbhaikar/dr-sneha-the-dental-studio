## 6. Review engine (target 10–15 a month)

1. **QR cards** with the direct review link (GBP → Ask for reviews → copy link) at reception and each chair.
2. **Same-evening WhatsApp** to every patient after treatment:
   > Hi {Name}, thank you for visiting Dr. Sneha-The Dental Studio today. If you have a minute, a short Google review helps other families in Wadgaon Sheri find us: {link}. Thank you! — Dr. Sneha Sardar
3. Ask, if they are comfortable, to mention the treatment. Never offer discounts, gifts or contests for reviews, and never ask only satisfied patients (review gating) — both violate Google policy.
4. **Reply to every review within 48 h.** Mention a treatment or area only if the reviewer did (patient confidentiality).
   - Positive: "Thank you, {Name}! We're glad your {treatment they named} at our Wadgaon Sheri clinic felt comfortable. See you at your next check-up. — Dr. Sneha Sardar and team"
   - Neutral/negative: "Thank you for the feedback, {Name}. We'd like to understand what happened — please call the clinic so Dr. Sneha can speak with you personally." (Never discuss clinical details publicly.)
5. Track monthly: new reviews, average rating, % replied, % mentioning a treatment.
